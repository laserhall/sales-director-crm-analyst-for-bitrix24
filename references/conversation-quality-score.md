# Conversation quality score

Use a 100-point score to review sales conversations consistently. Customize weights for the business.

## Default 100-point model

| Dimension | Points | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Response speed | 15 | timely first response and no unanswered client questions |
| Contact structure | 10 | greeting, role, clear conversation flow |
| Discovery quality | 20 | customer need, use case, constraints, timing collected |
| Qualification | 15 | budget, authority, decision process, size, repeat potential |
| Product/service fit | 15 | manager asks technical fit questions and offers relevant options |
| Commercial handling | 10 | price/proposal explained with scope, assumptions, alternatives |
| Next step clarity | 10 | specific action, owner, date/time, channel |
| CRM discipline | 5 | stage/activity/notes reflect reality |

## Scoring rules

- Score only what is visible in CRM/dialog evidence.
- Do not punish the manager for data the system cannot access.
- If message text is not available, score communication dimensions as `not enough evidence` instead of guessing.
- Use the score to coach behavior, not to shame people.

## Quick interpretation

- 85-100: strong sales conversation, minor improvements.
- 70-84: workable but with gaps that may reduce conversion.
- 50-69: material process gaps; manager coaching needed.
- Below 50: high risk; immediate review and recovery action.
