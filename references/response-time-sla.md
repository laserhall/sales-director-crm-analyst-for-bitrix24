# Response-time SLA

These SLA values are generic starting points. They must be customized per business, working hours, channel, deal size, seasonality, and customer expectations.

## Generic SLA draft

| Event | Default target | Review if |
|---|---:|---|
| First response to a new inbound lead | 5-15 minutes during working hours | no response within same business day |
| Missed call callback | 15-30 minutes during working hours | no callback logged |
| Clarifying question after incomplete request | same business day | manager quotes without key specs |
| Proposal/calculation after complete requirements | same business day or stated deadline | no proposal activity |
| Follow-up after proposal | next business day | no follow-up within 24 business hours |
| Customer silent after follow-up | 2-3 business days | no second touch or close/lost decision |
| Active deal without planned next action | 1-2 business days | no activity scheduled |
| Open deal with no activity | 7 days | review as stuck |

## SLA report fields

- entity ID;
- stage;
- assigned manager;
- last client activity time;
- last manager response time;
- planned next activity;
- breach type;
- recommended action.

## Important customization questions

- What are working hours?
- Which channels are high-priority?
- Which deal values require faster escalation?
- Which stages tolerate longer waiting?
- What is the normal proposal preparation time?
- Which industries/customers have tender-like decision cycles?
