# Follow-up playbooks

Follow-up is a sales-management discipline: every live deal needs a clear next touch, reason, and deadline.

## After proposal

Goal: confirm receipt, resolve questions, and agree decision timing.

Checklist:

- confirm the customer received the proposal;
- ask whether scope and assumptions are clear;
- clarify decision timing;
- offer a specific next step;
- schedule CRM activity.

Template pattern:

```text
Hi, checking that you received the proposal for {scope}. Is the scope clear, or should I adjust any option? If it works, let's agree the next step: {specific action/date}.
```

## Client went silent

Goal: restart the conversation without pressure.

Pattern:

```text
Hi, I am following up on {request/proposal}. Should we keep this option active, change the scope, or pause it for now? I can help with {specific next action}.
```

## Missing requirements

Goal: collect the minimum data needed for a valid offer.

Pattern:

```text
To calculate this correctly, I still need {missing specs}. Please send them, and I will prepare {proposal/calculation} by {time}.
```

## Larger deal / complex decision

Goal: identify decision process and stakeholders.

Pattern:

```text
For this scope, it is better to align requirements before final pricing. Who else should be involved in the decision, and when do you need the final proposal?
```

## Lost recovery

Goal: learn reason and keep future opportunity open.

Pattern:

```text
Thanks for the update. Could you share what was the deciding factor: price, timing, scope, or another reason? It will help us offer a better option next time.
```
